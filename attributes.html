Attribute / SEA Planner
Plan your six core attributes from jewelry, food / buffs, and armor / SEAs, and see how diminishing returns reshape them. Past the soft cap of 250, each point is worth less — effective = (750 × total) ÷ (500 + total). Stacking one stat sky-high is wasteful; spreading points keeps them near 1:1. Your SEA/armor numbers can auto-import from the Order Form, or you can enter them by hand. Adapted from Fez's Attribute/SEA Calculator (SWG Restoration).
